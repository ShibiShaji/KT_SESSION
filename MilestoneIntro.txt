# MILESTONE — FEATURE INTRODUCTION & UI WALKTHROUGH

## 1. WHAT IS MILESTONE? (THE CORE PURPOSE IN SIMPLE WORDS)

Milestone is the child portfolio and developmental growth tracking feature of the nursery application.

In early childhood education, nurseries track each child's learning journey across standardized developmental milestones (cognitive, physical, language, social-emotional).
Milestone provides:
- A visual portfolio of a child's learning progress and developmental achievements.
- Clear separation between Academic and Non-Academic milestones.
- Evidence-based logging: every milestone is backed by real classroom photos, videos, and mapped assessment standards/behaviours.
- Transparent reporting for parents to celebrate and review their child's achievements at home.

---

## 2. WHO USES IT? (USER ROLES & WHAT THEY DO)

### 1. Parent (PMS App User)
- Selects from their enrolled children on the Milestone screen.
- Explores their child's portfolio timeline and filter by Academic or Non-Academic achievements.
- Filters achievements by specific dates or calendar ranges.
- Taps any milestone to view detailed teacher observations, learning standards, and attached classroom photos or videos.

### 2. Nursery Teacher / Staff (NMS App User)
- Navigates through the Classroom student roster and selects a student under the Milestone flow.
- Reviews student milestone history to assess developmental readiness and curriculum progress.
- Accesses direct student messaging via the in-card "Chat" shortcut to discuss specific milestones with parents.

## 3. WHAT ARE THE MAIN VIEWS? (UI EXPLAINED IN SIMPLE WORDS)

### View 1: Child Selection Screen (milestoneScreen.dart)
This is the entry screen for parents from their dashboard.

- Child Photo Grid:
  Displays a 2-column grid of all enrolled children belonging to the logged-in parent.
- Child Card Information:
  Shows child photo avatar, full name, class grade, section, and nursery name.
- Tap Action:
  Tapping a child card passes the child details (`KidDetailsModel`) and opens their dedicated milestone portfolio screen.

*(For Nursery Staff, this entry happens via `classroomStudentList.dart`, where teachers pick a student from their assigned classroom).*

---

### View 2: Milestone Portfolio & Listing Screen (milestoneChildCardDetails.dart)
This is the core portfolio feed showing all milestones logged for the selected child.

- Top Header Card (Staff View):
  Displays child avatar, full name (English / Arabic), student code, grade, section, and an action button to open a direct chat.

- Date Filter Bar:
  • Calendar Icon (`FilterBasedOnDates`): Allows filtering by a single date or custom date range.
  • Selected Date Display: Shows formatted active date chip with a "Clear Filters" button.

- Category Filter Toggle Buttons:
  A horizontal pill toggle bar to filter achievements:
  • "All": Shows all recorded milestones.
  • "Academic": Filters for academic milestones (`milestoneType == 1`).
  • "Non-Academic": Filters for physical/social/creative milestones (`milestoneType != 1`).

- Milestone Card Feed:
  Each card in the feed represents a milestone event:
  • Header: Milestone title (in English or Arabic), a separator dot, and creation date (`dd/MM/yyyy`).
  • Category Pill: Color-coded tag (purple for Academic, blue for Non-Academic).
  • Description: Full teacher observation notes.
  • Media Thumbnail Carousel: Horizontal scrolling preview of attached photos or video thumbnails.
  • Tap Action: Tapping the card opens the detailed Bottom Sheet breakdown.

---

### View 3: Detailed Breakdown Bottom Sheet (milestoneChildCardDetails.dart)
Tapping any milestone card slides up a comprehensive modal bottom sheet.

- Milestone Summary:
  Displays the milestone title, category pill, and complete observation description.

- Learning Standards & Behaviours Accordions:
  Learning behaviours are automatically grouped by Assessment Standards:
  • Accordion Header: Standard category title (e.g. "Physical Development", "Communication Skills") with an expand/collapse arrow.
  • Expanded List: Individual developmental behaviours and teacher assessments mapped under that standard.

- Attachments Grid (Photos & Videos):
  • Displays all supporting media in a 3-column photo grid.
  • Video items feature a video play thumbnail badge.
  • Tapping any media fetches secure presigned URLs from S3 and launches:
    - Fullscreen Photo Viewer (`MilestoneFullscreenViewer`) for high-resolution images.
    - Embedded Video Player (`VideoPlayerScreen`) for `.mp4` video recordings.

## 4. KEY HIGHLIGHTS TO SAY IN YOUR KT (CHEAT SHEET)

1. Dual-Audience Unified UI:
   "Both parents and nursery teachers view the portfolio using the exact same detail screen (milestoneChildCardDetails.dart), but the backend adapts endpoints dynamically depending on whether isNurseryUser is true or false."

2. Multi-Media Evidence:
   "Milestones aren't just dry text scores. They are visual portfolios with direct photo and video evidence fetched securely via presigned S3 URLs."

3. Structured Early-Years Standards:
   "Inside the bottom sheet, observations are grouped under specific curriculum standards (Assessment Standard → Behaviours), giving parents deep insight into their child's cognitive and physical milestones."

4. Dynamic Categorization & Date Range Filtering:
   "Parents can toggle between Academic and Non-Academic milestones, or use the calendar filter to review milestones achieved during a specific week or month."
