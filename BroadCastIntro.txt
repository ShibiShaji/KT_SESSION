# BROADCAST — FEATURE INTRODUCTION & UI WALKTHROUGH

## 1. WHAT IS BROADCAST? (THE CORE PURPOSE IN SIMPLE WORDS)

Broadcast is the official one-to-many announcement and compliance broadcasting system in the nursery application.

While regular Logbook is a private 1-on-1 conversation between a staff member and a parent, Broadcast allows authorized nursery staff to push an official circular, emergency alert, or consent form to entire classrooms, sections, or the entire nursery at once.

Key distinction:
- It is NOT just a notification.
- It is an interactive, legally tracked notice where parents must formally respond (Accept / Reject / Acknowledge).
- Staff can monitor real-time compliance metrics (e.g. 24 Accepted, 2 Rejected, 4 Pending).

---

## 2. WHO USES IT? (USER ROLES & WHAT THEY DO)

### 1. Principal / Nursery Admin (NMS Staff)
- Has nursery-wide broadcasting authority.
- Pushes school closures, holiday circulars, administrative policies, and event announcements under the Principal tab.
- Tracks nursery-wide parent participation and acknowledgment rates.

### 2. Teacher (NMS Staff)
- Creates classroom-level announcements under the Teacher tab.
- Broadcasts upcoming field trips, project material requests, or weekly curriculum summaries to their assigned classes.
- Tracks which parents approved permission slips for upcoming class events.

### 3. Nurse (NMS Staff)
- Sends medical advisories under the Nurse tab.
- Broadcasts seasonal health alerts (e.g. flu outbreaks, lice checks) and vaccination consent forms to parents.
- Identifies parents who have not responded to urgent health notices.

### 4. Parent (PMS App User)
- Receives the broadcast notice embedded directly inside the child's Logbook conversation timeline.
- Reads the notice with its official badge and role tag.
- For Consent/Mandatory notices, taps "Accept" or "Reject", immediately recording their choice and timestamp.

## 3. WHAT ARE THE MAIN VIEWS? (UI EXPLAINED IN SIMPLE WORDS)

### View 1: Broadcast Listing Dashboard (broadcastInitial.dart)
This is the staff's management screen showing all past and active broadcasts.

- Role Tabs (CustomMainTabBar):
  Tabs for Principal, Teacher, and Nurse. Switching tabs filters broadcasts by author role.

- Search & Filter Controls:
  • Search input: Filter by broadcast subject or content keyword.
  • Filter Icon: Filter by classroom/section or specific date.

- Broadcast Cards (BroadcastItem):
  Each broadcast card displays:
  • Title & Subtitle: Subject line and message preview.
  • Megaphone Icon: Official announcement badge.
  • Timestamp: Date created.
  • Live 3-Color Metric Badges:
    - 🔵 Blue Badge: Number of parents who Accepted.
    - 🟠 Orange Badge: Number of parents who Rejected.
    - 🟡 Yellow/Amber Badge: Number of parents Pending / Not Responded.

- Floating Action Button (+):
  Visible to staff with broadcast permissions to open the composer.

---

### View 2: Broadcast Composer & Audience Selector (broadcastNurseryPrincipal.dart)
This is the creation screen where staff drafts and targets the broadcast.

- Role Selector: Choose sending role (Principal, Teacher, or Nurse).
- Target Audience Hierarchy:
  • Nursery selection.
  • Classroom / Section selection (multi-select checkmarks).
  • Optional: Select individual students within classes.
- Broadcast Message Type:
  • General: Standard announcement (read-only notice).
  • Consent: Requires parents to click Accept or Reject (e.g. field trips).
  • Mandatory: Requires mandatory acknowledgment before dismissal.
- Content Fields:
  • Subject line.
  • Description / Reason text area.
- Send Button:
  Validates fields and calls the API to create the conversation records across all targeted students.

---

### View 3: Live Response & Parent Compliance Tracker (broadcastResponse.dart)
Tapping any broadcast card opens this screen to see exactly who responded.

- 3 Status Tabs:
  • Accepted Tab: List of students whose parents clicked Accept.
  • Rejected Tab: List of students whose parents clicked Reject.
  • Pending Tab: List of students whose parents have not answered yet.
- Student Cards:
  Displays student photo, name, class, and parent contact details for quick follow-up calls or reminders.
- Class Filter:
  Allows staff to filter the response list by a single section (e.g. "Show only Class 1 responses").

---

### View 4: Parent Interactive Chat Bubble (broadCastBubble.dart)
The parent does NOT have a separate broadcast inbox—the broadcast lands inside their child's Logbook chat.

- Highlighted Card:
  Clearly stands out from normal messages with a colored border and megaphone icon.
- Content Area:
  Displays the sender role, subject line, full announcement, and timestamp.
- Action Buttons (for Consent / Mandatory messages):
  • "Accept" button (green/blue styling).
  • "Reject" button (outline/red styling).
- Dynamic Status Change:
  Once the parent taps Accept or Reject, the buttons vanish and are replaced by a permanent badge ("Accepted on [Date/Time]" or "Rejected on [Date/Time]").

---

## 4. KEY HIGHLIGHTS TO SAY IN YOUR KT (CHEAT SHEET)

1. Seamless Integration with Logbook:
   "Parents don't need a separate broadcast screen. Announcements appear directly in their child's chat timeline as distinct, actionable cards."

2. Auditable Two-Way Compliance:
   "Unlike WhatsApp or email where staff have no idea if parents read a circular, Broadcast has built-in audit metrics: staff can see exact counts for Accepted, Rejected, and Pending in real-time."

3. Granular Targeting:
   "Staff can broadcast at multiple levels: nursery-wide, to a specific grade, to a specific section, or to a custom set of individual students."

4. Unified Data Architecture:
   "Under the hood, Broadcasts are special conversation records with isConsent: true and messageType flags. When a parent taps Accept or Reject, it posts a reply that updates the central audit log."
